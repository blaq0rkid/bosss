The Resilient Talent Xchange (RTX)

From Transactional Staffing to Workforce Architecture

The RTX Philosophy

The Resilient Talent Xchange (RTX) represents BOSSS's proprietary methodology for identifying and deploying high-value human capital in the face of 21st-century economic and technological disruptions.

We focus on Human-plus-AI symbiosis, where automation manages repetitive patterns and RTX Talent manages system disruptions. This isn't about replacing humans with technology—it's about positioning the right humans in roles where their resilience, adaptability, and judgment create maximum value.

The Grit Scorecard

A proprietary evaluation metric to vet candidates based on non-traditional high-performance indicators

Adaptability

Capacity to pivot roles within automated environments. Can you shift from oversight to manual intervention when systems fail?

Composure

Performance stability under logistics-chain pressure. Do you maintain clarity during Q4 surge, port congestion, or equipment failure?

Historical Tenacity

Evidence of overcoming professional or personal adversity. Veterans, career-changers, and re-entry populations often score highest.
