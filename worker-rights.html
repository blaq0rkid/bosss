Your Rights as a Worker

Annual Workplace Rights Notice (California SB 446 Compliance)

BOSSS Prioritizes Your Protection

In accordance with California Law (SB 446, effective 2026), all talent placed by Black Orchid Specialized Staffing Solutions are entitled to full disclosure of rights regarding workplace safety, fair compensation, and AI-augmented employment decisions.

Retaliation Protection

You cannot be penalized, demoted, or terminated for reporting safety concerns, wage discrepancies, or workplace violations. California law provides robust protections for whistleblowers.

  • Report safety hazards without fear of job loss
  • Question wage calculations or missing pay
  • Request accommodations for disabilities
  • File complaints with state labor authorities

Workers' Compensation

Coverage is provided from Day 1 of any BOSSS assignment. If you are injured on the job, you have the right to medical care and wage replacement.

  • Immediate medical treatment for work-related injuries
  • Compensation for lost wages during recovery
  • Protection from employer retaliation for filing claims
  • Right to choose your treating physician after 30 days

Report injuries immediately: compliance@bosssllc.com

Fair AI Review

You have the right to request a manual human audit of any AI-driven screening decision that affects your employment, promotion, or termination.

  • Request explanation of AI-driven decisions
  • Challenge automated rejections or scoring
  • Receive human review within 10 business days
  • Access your candidate data and Grit Scorecard results

Request AI review: compliance@bosssllc.com
