In a perfect world, automation runs smoothly 24/7. But as anyone in logistics knows, the world isn't perfect. When a sensor fails on an electric crane or a software update glitches in the warehouse management system, you don't need a robot. You need a human who doesn't panic.

This is why high-growth logistics environments are pivoting their talent acquisition strategies. It’s no longer enough to find someone who knows how to operate the software; you need someone who has the tenacity to solve the problem when the software stops working.
