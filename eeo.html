Equal Employment Opportunity

Our Commitment to Diversity, Inclusion & Fairness

Effective Date: April 3, 2026

EEO Policy Statement

At Black Orchid Specialized Staffing Solutions (BOSSS), we believe that resilience and grit are found in every background. We are committed to a policy of equal employment opportunity for all applicants and employees.

1 Our Commitment

BOSSS provides equal employment opportunities to all individuals without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetics, protected veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local laws.

2 Scope of Policy

This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including:

Talent Acquisition

  • Recruiting and hiring
  • Placements and assignments

Career Development

  • Promotion and termination
  • Compensation and training

Workplace Culture

  • Social and recreational programs
  • All other terms and conditions of employment

3 Workforce Architects of Inclusion

As "Workforce Architects," we specifically value the diverse perspectives that high-grit, resilient professionals bring to the logistics and supply chain industry. We actively seek to build a diverse talent pool that reflects the Port of Stockton community and the evolving needs of zero-emission logistics.

4 Reasonable Accommodations

BOSSS is committed to the full inclusion of all qualified individuals. As part of this commitment, we will ensure that persons with disabilities are provided reasonable accommodations.

Need an accommodation?

If a reasonable accommodation is needed to participate in the job application or interview process, please contact us at:

info@bosssllc.com

5 AI Transparency & Fairness

In accordance with California 2026 standards, while BOSSS utilizes AI-assisted tools for candidate matching, these systems are regularly audited for bias to ensure that our technology supports—and does not hinder—our commitment to equal opportunity.

Your Rights Regarding AI

Candidates always have the right to request a manual human review of any AI-influenced screening decision.
