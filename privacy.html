Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Name, email, phone number, mailing address
  • Professional Data: Resume, work history, skills, certifications
  • Application Data: Job preferences, availability, compensation expectations
  • Behavioral Data: Website usage, email interactions, communication preferences
  • AI-Generated Insights: Skill assessments, grit profile scores, RTX compatibility ratings

How We Use Your Information

  • Matching candidates with job opportunities
  • Communicating about placements, assignments, and opportunities
  • Improving our RTX methodology and AI-assisted matching systems
  • Complying with legal obligations (SB 446, CCPA, etc.)
  • Sending marketing communications (with your consent)

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your information only in these circumstances:

  • Client Companies: When you apply for or accept a placement, we share relevant professional information with the hiring company
  • Service Providers: Background check vendors, payroll processors, and email service providers (all CCPA-compliant)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel who need it to perform their jobs. We conduct annual third-party security audits.

AI Transparency

In compliance with California's 2026 AI disclosure requirements, BOSSS provides full transparency about how artificial intelligence is used in our hiring and placement processes.

How We Use AI

  • Resume Screening: AI parses resumes to extract skills, experience, and qualifications
  • Skill Matching: Algorithms match candidate profiles with job requirements
  • Grit Assessment: AI analyzes responses to behavioral questions to estimate resilience traits
  • Predictive Analytics: Models predict candidate success likelihood based on historical placement data

Your AI Rights

Under California law, you have the right to:

  • Know when AI is used in hiring decisions affecting you
  • Request a human review of any AI-influenced decision
  • Understand the factors AI considers when evaluating your application
  • Opt out of certain AI-based assessments (though this may limit available opportunities)
