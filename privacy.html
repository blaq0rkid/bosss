AI Transparency & Ethics

How BOSSS Uses AI

Black Orchid Specialized Staffing Solutions utilizes AI-augmented screening for operational efficiency. In accordance with California transparency laws (SB 243/AB 1898), all candidates retain the right to request a manual human review of any automated employment decision.

What AI Does at BOSSS

  • Initial resume screening for keyword matching and experience relevance
  • Grit Scorecard preliminary assessment based on historical patterns
  • Scheduling optimization for interviews and placement coordination
  • Market trend analysis for workforce demand forecasting

What AI Does NOT Do

  • Make final hiring decisions without human review
  • Access protected class information (age, race, disability status)
  • Determine compensation rates or salary negotiations
  • Replace human judgment in RTX Activation placements

Your Right to Human Review

If you believe an AI system made an error in your candidacy evaluation, you may request a manual human review at any time. Our compliance team will respond within 10 business days.

Data Freedom: Your Choice

One-Click Data Purge

Your data, your choice. As a pioneer in AI-driven staffing, BOSSS provides a "One-Click Purge" option. At any time, you may request the complete deletion of your screening data, resume history, and Grit Scorecard from our active AI training models. No questions asked.

Important: Data purge requests are permanent and cannot be reversed. You will need to resubmit all information if you wish to be considered for future opportunities.

Request Data Purge

What Gets Deleted

  • Resume and application materials
  • Grit Scorecard results and assessments
  • Interview notes and evaluations
  • AI training data derived from your profile
  • Email correspondence (subject to legal holds)

What We Keep (Legal Requirements)

  • Tax documentation (7 years)
  • Workers' compensation records
  • Payroll history for placed candidates
  • Compliance audit trails
  • Records subject to active legal proceedings

Additional Privacy Protections

CCPA/CPRA Compliance

BOSSS complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Know if we sell or share your personal information (we don't)
  • Access your personal information
  • Delete your personal information (with exceptions)
  • Opt-out of automated decision-making

Data Security

We utilize industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3), multi-factor authentication for staff access, and annual third-party security audits. All candidate data is stored on SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure.

Digital Accessibility

BOSSS is committed to digital accessibility. This site is designed to be WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliant to ensure an inclusive experience for all users, including those using assistive technologies.

Our compliance team is here to help you understand and exercise your privacy rights.
