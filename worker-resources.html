Safety & Health (Cal/OSHA)

Your Right to a Safe Workplace

BOSSS is committed to maintaining Green Port Certified safety standards across all client sites. Every RTX team member has the right to work in an environment free from recognized hazards.

How to Report a Workplace Hazard or Injury

IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED: If you witness or experience a workplace hazard or injury, take the following steps immediately:

  1. Ensure immediate safety - Remove yourself from danger if possible
  2. Notify your on-site supervisor at the client location
  3. Contact the BOSSS Safety Hotline: (209) 917-8588
  4. For medical emergencies: Call 911 first, then notify BOSSS
  5. Document the incident with photos if safe to do so

California SB 446 - Temporary Worker Rights

California law (SB 446) requires staffing agencies to provide temporary workers with written notice of:

  • Actual wages and benefits for each assignment
  • Details about the client company and job location
  • Expected duration of assignment
  • Any fees deducted from worker pay
  • Safety requirements and workers' compensation coverage

BOSSS provides this information through individual assignment letters and our Worker Rights page.

Pay & Benefits Information

California Paid Sick Leave (PSL)

Under California's Healthy Workplaces, Healthy Families Act, all BOSSS employees are entitled to paid sick leave.

Key Details:

  • Accrual Rate: You earn at least 1 hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked
  • Minimum Availability: Employers must provide at least 24 hours (3 days) of paid sick leave per year
  • When You Can Use It: For your own illness, preventive care, or to care for a family member
  • Job Protection: You cannot be terminated or retaliated against for using paid sick leave
  • Notice: Your current PSL balance appears on your pay stub

Harassment-Free Workplace

Zero-Tolerance Policy

BOSSS maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination of any kind.

We are committed to providing a work environment free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This commitment applies to all employees, temporary workers, clients, vendors, and any other individuals encountered in the course of employment.

What Constitutes Harassment?

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:

  • Verbal harassment: Unwelcome comments, jokes, slurs, or epithets based on protected characteristics
  • Physical harassment: Unwanted touching, assault, or physically intimidating behavior
  • Visual harassment: Offensive images, cartoons, or gestures
  • Sexual harassment: Unwanted sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or other verbal/physical conduct of a sexual nature
  • Retaliation: Adverse action taken against someone for reporting harassment or discrimination
